Refund and cancellation policy

How cancellations and refunds work for our monthly plans and services.

Last updated: September 2026

Cancelling a plan

You can cancel a monthly plan at any time by emailing us before your next billing date. Your plan continues until the end of the month you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Setup fee

The one-time setup fee is not refundable once setup work has started.

Monthly fees

Monthly fees already paid are not refunded for the current month. If we are unable to deliver the videos included in your plan for reasons on our side, we will either deliver them in the following month or refund the value of the undelivered videos, as you prefer.

Other services

For ads, websites, automation and other quoted work, cancellation and refund terms are agreed in writing before the work starts. Money paid directly to ad platforms such as Meta or Google is not refundable by us.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.
